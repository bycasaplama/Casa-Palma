Our philosophy
"The right room changes everything."
You walk in curious.
You leave belonging.
The belief
When you put the right people together, with intention, with taste, with care, something happens that you can't manufacture or replicate. You just feel it.
The approach
Less is more. Every detail is deliberate. The ribbon on your wrist, the soft music, the person you met by the door, nothing is accidental. Casa Palma designs for feeling, not spectacle.
The values
Intimacy over scale, quality over quantity, presence over performance, connection over networking, the right moment over the right number.

05 — Questions
Everything you
need to know.
What is a Drop?+
A Drop is a curated experience — intimate, intentional, and designed for connection. Each one is different. All of them are worth it.
How do I get the address?+
Fill in the form and you're in. The address arrives straight to your inbox on the day of the Drop.
Is it free?+
Some Drops are ticketed, some are complimentary. Details are always shared when you sign up.
Can I bring a friend?+
Each person signs up individually — that's how we keep the room curated. But if they belong here, send them to casapalmadrops.com.
What should I wear?+
Come as you are. The only dress code is good energy.
What if I can't make it?+
Life happens. Just let us know 24 hours before and we'll save your spot for the next Drop.
